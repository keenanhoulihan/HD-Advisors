# Editing your website

Kate, this is everything you need to update the High Definition Advisors site yourself. No code, nothing to install.

## Logging in

1. Go to your website and add `/admin` to the end of the address (for example `yoursite.com/admin`).
2. Log in with your email. The first time, use the invite email Tina sends you to set your password.
3. You'll see your actual website on the right and an editing panel on the left.

## Making a change

1. Use the menu (top left) to pick a page: **Home page**, **About page**, **Approach page**, **Contact page**, or **Every page**.
2. In the left panel, click the section you want to change, like "Who we are" or "Experience timeline."
3. Click into any box and type. The page on the right updates as you type, so you can see exactly how it will look.
4. Happy with it? Click **Save** at the bottom left. Your site updates on its own in about a minute or two.
5. Changed your mind before saving? Click **Reset** and everything goes back.

## Lists (adding, removing, reordering)

Some sections are lists, like the experience timeline, the four problems, or the roadmap phases.

- **Add an item:** click the orange **+** next to the list name, then fill in the new item.
- **Remove an item:** click the small trash can next to it.
- **Reorder:** drag the dotted handle on the left of an item up or down.

## Changing your headshot

1. Open **About page**, then **Kate**.
2. Click the photo, then upload a new one or pick one you've uploaded before.
3. Update **Headshot description** so it still describes the photo.
4. Save.

A square photo works best. It shows in a circle on a light lavender background.

## What's where

| You want to change | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| The sentence under "Clarity for Complex Growth" | Every page > Home hero sentence |
| Your email, phone, or website | Every page > Contact details |
| The "Clarity starts with a conversation" box at the bottom of pages | Every page > Closing call to action |
| Your bio, title, or headshot | About page > Kate |
| Your past roles | About page > Experience timeline |
| The big statements between sections | Each page > Motto statements |
| The four lenses or the roadmap phases | Approach page |
| The contact form choices or "thank you" message | Contact page |

## What you can't break

Colors, fonts, the line artwork, and the layout are locked. You can only change words, lists, and your photo, so the site always looks on brand. If something ever looks off after saving, just change it back and save again.

## Good to know

- Keep the four lenses and four phases in their current order. Each one has its own artwork.
- Use a different motto on each page so they don't repeat.
- Every save is kept in history, so nothing is ever lost. If you need an old version back, ask Keenan.
